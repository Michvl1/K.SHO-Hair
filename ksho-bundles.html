FAQ

Your questions

How many packs do I need?

A full head takes 8–10 packs. For bigger or longer styles you may need a few extra, which you can buy as single packs. If you're booked in with me, I'll confirm what you need before you order.

Which length should I choose?

12" is bob length, 16" is bra-strap length, 22" is lower-back length and 24" is bum length.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are posted within 2 working days by tracked delivery. We ship across the UK and internationally — international delivery times and costs are shown at checkout.
