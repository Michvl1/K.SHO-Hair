Get started

Bring in your Stripe sales

  1. In Stripe, go to Payments (or Transactions) and click Export.
  2. Pick a date range, keep the default columns, and download the CSV.
  3. Drop the file here. Upload again any time; orders you already have are updated, never duplicated.

Tip: in the export's column picker, add the Shipping and Customer email columns so addresses and emails come through.
